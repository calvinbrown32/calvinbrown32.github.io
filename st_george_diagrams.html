Improving Accessibility and Passenger Experience Using GTFS Pathways and Internal Mapping Data Format (IMDF)

Using GTFS Pathways and Apple’s Indoor Mapping Data Format (IMDF) as a shared data foundation for architectural and information design, wayfinding content production, and accessible passenger navigation at the St. George Ferry Terminal.

Improving Accessibility and Passenger Experience

GTFS Pathways and the Indoor Mapping Data Format (IMDF) provide structured representations of complex, multi-modal, multi-level transit facilities. By abstracting physical spaces, pedestrian pathways, and connections between transportation services, these specifications make it possible to analyze and communicate the relationships that shape passenger movement through a terminal.

This shared data foundation can inform architectural and wayfinding design, support accessible and real-time passenger routing, and enable the automated production and updating of maps, graphics, and other passenger information products. Open specifications also allow developers to incorporate this information into third-party applications, extending its potential to improve the passenger experience across multiple platforms.
